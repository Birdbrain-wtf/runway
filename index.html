How the sums work

Live balances, estimated selling costs and 1,000 replays of past DOT price moves. Replays are not forecasts.

Spending and day-one sales
  • Everything starts in DOT. By default all of it is sold to USDC on day one and spending is paid from that USDC. Any share kept in DOT is paid to teams, who sell it each month at that month's price to cover their costs. The money has run out when a month's spending cannot be covered in full.
  • Sold to USDC on day one converts that share of the starting DOT, and the same share of any DOT that arrives later at the price on the day it lands.
  • DOT price the plan needs is the steady price at which the money just lasts the whole plan.
Selling costs and exclusions
  • Selling costs come from the live order book of the market you pick: Kraken (DOT/USDC, DOT/USD, DOT/USDT), Coinbase (DOT/USD) or Binance (DOT/USDT, DOT/USDC). A sale is walked down the bids from the best price, so a big sale fills lower, and the exchange's taker fee is added. Kraken Pro's fee follows the tier that size earns: the day-one sale by the DOT deposited, a team's monthly sale by that month's trading. Coinbase is charged at its 0.50% entry rate for UK and EU accounts, since its full tier table could not be read, so a big Coinbase sale is shown slightly dearer than it would be. US accounts pay 0.90%. Binance is charged its 0.10% regular rate. Today's price is the middle of Kraken's DOT/USDC market, between the best bid and the best offer, read every 15 minutes. The daily closes are used only for the replays. Deposit and withdrawal fees are not counted, nor is converting dollars or USDT to USDC, nor the price moving while a big sale is spread over days. DOT still held is valued at the market price before selling costs.
How replays work
  • Replay history builds 1,000 price paths. Each month takes a 30-day DOT move picked at random from real daily closes in the window you choose. Swings only removes the window's average drift, so the middle path stays near today's price and only the size of the swings comes from history. Keep the trend leaves the drift in, which repeats whatever DOT did over that window. The draws are seeded, so the same plan gives the same answer.
  • 9 in 10 replays means the money lasted at least that long in 900 of the 1,000 paths. It describes past swings, not a forecast.
Data sources and the 10M DOT commitment
  • Pot figures come from Birdbrain's Kusama treasury reader, read at a finalised block on Kusama Asset Hub. DOT prices are daily closes in dollars from Yahoo Finance, refreshed every morning for KSM and DOT and published with their checks at github.com/Birdbrain-wtf/ksm-dot.
  • The 10 million DOT is the Web3 Foundation's commitment under referendum 498, enacted 8 March 2025, "for the sole benefit of Kusama". Only what has been moved into a pot shows on chain. The rest is not in Kusama's treasury, so this page cannot read it.
